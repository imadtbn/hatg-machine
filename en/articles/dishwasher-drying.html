Dishwasher Doesn't Dry Dishes: Common Causes and Practical Solutions

Dishwashers August 10, 2026 6 min read
Safety alert

Disconnect power and shut off the water source before cleaning or inspecting. Do not open gas, refrigeration, or high-voltage circuits without a qualified technician.

Some eco programs may leave more water droplets because they use less heat or time. So distinguish between normal program behaviour and a fault in the heating or ventilation. Plastic also retains water more than glass and metal.

Improving results without repair

Load dishes at an angle to allow water to drain and do not nest cups inside each other. Make sure the spray arms rotate freely and the filter is clean. Use the recommended rinse aid and set the dosage according to water hardness and the model manual. Open the door slightly after the cycle ends if the function is allowed and safe.

Signs of a heating fault

If dishes remain very cold with an absence of steam on a heated program, or detergent residues appear with a recurring problem, the cause may be a heater, a temperature sensor, or a control board. Do not disassemble the heater; disconnect the appliance and call a technician, especially if the circuit breaker tripped or there was a burning smell.

Clean the filter periodically because poor water flow affects cleaning and drying together. When an error code appears, write down the code and the model number before restarting so it can be matched with the manufacturer's manual or a fault database.
