Why is the washing machine vibrating? Causes and fixes before calling a technician

Washing machine faults August 9, 2026 6 min read
Safety alert

Disconnect power and shut off the water source before cleaning or inspecting. Do not open gas, refrigeration, or high-voltage circuits without a qualified technician.

A light vibration at the start of the spin cycle is normal, but the machine moving or hitting the wall is not. Start with causes that don't require tools, because an unbalanced load or an uneven floor can mimic a mechanical fault.

Three quick checks

  1. Stop the cycle and redistribute large items. Do not wash a single blanket by itself if the manual recommends balancing it with other items.
  2. Place a spirit level on the top if available, and adjust the four feet until they sit firmly on the floor, then tighten the lock nuts.
  3. Make sure the transit bolts are removed on new washers; leaving them in place causes severe vibration and damage.

Leave a small gap from the wall and do not place the appliance on a flexible surface. If you hear grinding or metallic knocking even with an empty drum, or water leaking from the bottom, do not continue to operate. The problem may be with the shock absorbers, bearings, or balance weight, and it requires a technician.

How to prevent recurrence?

Respect the rated capacity and don't overpack the drum, and use a spin speed appropriate for the fabric. Watch the washer during the first minutes of the spin after it's been moved or reinstalled, and note when the vibration appears: with a specific load or during every cycle.
