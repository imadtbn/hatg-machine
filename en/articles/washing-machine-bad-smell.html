Bad smell from the washing machine? Causes of mold, moisture and detergent residue

Washing machines August 9, 2026 7 min read
Safety alert

Disconnect power and shut off the water source before cleaning or inspecting. Do not open gas, refrigeration, or high-voltage circuits without a qualified technician.

A musty smell in the washer often appears when moisture and detergent residue remain in the gasket, the drawer, or the drum. This does not necessarily mean an electrical fault; start by drying and cleaning the parts according to the model manual, then monitor whether the smell returns after subsequent cycles.

Places where odors accumulate

Check the inner fold of the door gasket, the detergent drawer, the drawer drain openings, and the pump filter if user access is allowed. Remove coins, lint and small objects with a glove, and expect residual water to come out of the filter. Do not disassemble the pump or an internal hose without a manual or a technician.

Safe cleaning plan

  1. Disconnect power and shut off water if needed, and remove wet clothes immediately when the cycle ends.
  2. Wipe the gasket with a soft cloth and dry the inner fold, inspecting it for tears or trapped objects.
  3. Remove the detergent drawer and clean it with warm water, and dry its channels before putting it back.
  4. Run the tub-clean cycle or an empty cycle recommended by the manufacturer, and do not mix bleach with vinegar or any acid.
  5. Leave the door and drawer ajar after use if the model instructions and household ventilation allow it.

Detergent and load

Extra detergent dose does not mean cleaner results; it can leave a soapy film or residue that increases odors. Use a detergent suitable for the machine type and in the quantity stated in the manual, and avoid overfilling the drum because heavy loads prevent proper rinsing and drying.

When is the smell a sign of a fault?

Call a technician if the smell is accompanied by a leak, warmth at the plug, rubbing noises, standing water, or if the odor returns quickly despite cleaning the gasket, drawer and filter. A chemical smell or burning odor may indicate a different problem that requires stopping the machine immediately.
