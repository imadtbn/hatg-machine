How to save energy in household appliances without sacrificing performance?

Energy Saving August 2, 2026 8 min read
Safety alert

Disconnect power and shut off the water source before cleaning or inspecting. Do not open gas, refrigeration, or high-voltage circuits without a qualified technician.

Energy saving starts with choosing the right appliance and then operating it under the conditions it was designed for. A marketing label like "economical" is not enough; compare the consumption figures on the energy label or in the manual, because actual usage is affected by temperature, load size and number of cycles.

Four habits with a clear impact

  1. Run the washing machine and dishwasher with an appropriate load instead of repeating small cycles, while avoiding overloading that prevents water and air circulation.
  2. Clean filters, gaskets and vents; blockages force the fan, compressor or pump to run longer.
  3. Set the air conditioner to a comfortable temperature with doors and windows closed, and clean the filter regularly so airflow does not decrease.
  4. Leave ventilation space behind the refrigerator and do not place it near a direct heat source; a high ambient temperature increases compressor work.

How do you read the efficiency label?

The U.S. Federal Trade Commission explains that the EnergyGuide label shows the expected energy use of the appliance and compares it to similar models. Use the figure to compare appliances of the same class and size, and do not compare a small washer to a large air conditioner and conclude that one is more efficient.

The ENERGY STAR program also states that labeled products meet strict energy efficiency specifications set by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. The label is useful as an initial indicator, but check the model specifications and the consumption label before buying.

What not to do?

Do not turn the refrigerator or air conditioner on and off repeatedly to try to save energy, and do not always use short programs if they do not achieve the required cleaning. Removing a door gasket or running an air conditioner with a clogged filter can increase consumption and cause more expensive damage. Make saving the result of proper maintenance, not the result of disabling safety features.
