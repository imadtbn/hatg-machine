Most common dishwasher problems: causes and safe solutions

Dishwashers August 5, 2026 8 min read
Safety alert

Disconnect power and shut off the water source before cleaning or inspecting. Do not open gas, refrigeration, or high-voltage circuits without a qualified technician.

Before interpreting an error code, note the symptoms: is water not entering, not draining, or is the motor running without adequate spraying? The same code can mean different things between brands, so match the code with the model's manual on the manufacturer's website or the booklet.

Poor cleaning

Make sure the spray arms are not blocked by large items or a tall plate, then remove the arm and clean its holes with a thin plastic pick and rinse it. Clean the filter, use a detergent designed for dishwashers, and check that the water reaches the temperature required by the selected program. Do not use hand dishwashing liquid, as it causes excessive foaming.

Standing water at the bottom

Disconnect power, clean the filter and the pump area according to the manual, and check the drain hose for kinks. If the hose is connected to an air gap or a sink drain, the blockage may be outside the dishwasher. Do not put your hand into the pump area before ensuring there is no broken glass.

When does the code matter?

If a code related to a leak, overheating, or motor fault repeats after a single restart, stop the appliance and shut off the water. Do not bypass the leak sensor or disconnect wiring; these protections prevent further damage. Check the fault guide on this site with your model number, because the code alone is not a definitive diagnosis.
