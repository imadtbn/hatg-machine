How to Choose the Right Washing Machine? A Pre-Purchase Guide

Buying Guide August 6, 2026 8 min read
Safety alert

Disconnect power and shut off the water source before cleaning or inspecting. Do not open gas, refrigeration, or high-voltage circuits without a qualified technician.

The right washing machine is the one that fits the number of household members, the installation space, and your laundry habits—not the one with the largest number on the label. Start by measuring width, depth and height and the door opening path, and leave room for hoses, ventilation and maintenance.

Key criteria

CriterionPractical question
CapacityDoes it suit your average load without running half-empty or overfilling the drum?
EfficiencyWhat is the declared water and electricity consumption compared to appliances of the same size?
ProgramsAre there programs for delicates, economy, and quick wash without unnecessary extras?
ServiceAre service centers, spare parts, and a clear warranty available in your country?

ENERGY STAR indicates that the efficiency label is based on specific criteria, while the FTC's EnergyGuide explains how to compare expected consumption. Use these data together with operating and maintenance costs, not with the purchase price alone.

Questions to Ask the Seller

Ask about the warranty on the motor and control board, the cost of consumable parts, availability of technicians, and drain hose installation requirements. Request the full model number, because a single trade name may include different versions depending on the market. Keep the receipt, the user manual, and a photo of the label.
