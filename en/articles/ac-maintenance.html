Air conditioner maintenance before summer: steps to preserve cooling and reduce energy use

Air Conditioners August 4, 2026 7 min read
Safety alert

Disconnect power and shut off the water source before cleaning or inspecting. Do not open gas, refrigeration, or high-voltage circuits without a qualified technician.

Before running the air conditioner for long periods, inspect the parts that are accessible without removing the electrical cover. Reduced airflow or dust buildup on the filter reduces comfort and may increase run time. The refrigeration circuit, compressor, and wiring are not safe household tasks.

What you can do safely

Turn off the breaker, remove the washable filter, clean it with lukewarm water, and let it dry completely before reinstalling. Wipe the vents and the area around the indoor unit, and make sure no furniture blocks the airflow. For the outdoor unit, remove leaves and dust from around it while keeping proper clearance, and do not spray water on the electrical panel.

Signs you need an AC technician

Call a technician if you see repeated ice on the pipes, water leaking inside the room, a burning smell, a grinding noise, weak cooling despite a clean filter, or frequent breaker trips. Do not add refrigerant yourself; a low charge may be the result of a leak, and repairing the cause and measuring pressure require tools and qualification.

Principles from ENERGY STAR for saving energy at home recommend maintaining airflow and performing proper maintenance because efficient operation depends on the integrity of the whole system, not just lowering the thermostat setpoint.

More efficient operation

Close unused vents, use curtains that reduce solar load, and clean the filter according to local dust levels and the manufacturer's instructions. Do not set the temperature very low with windows open; this increases run time without a proportionate actual improvement.
